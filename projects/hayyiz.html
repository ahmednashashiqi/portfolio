Storage marketplace · Co-founder

Hayyiz

A Saudi marketplace where people and businesses can find storage space and owners can list unused rooms, garages, and other spaces.

Visit Hayyiz ↗
Role
Co-founder and developer
Period
Oct 2026–present
Platforms
Web, Android, and iOS

The problem

Someone looking for nearby storage needs to find a space that suits their items, location, and budget. Owners need a straightforward way to list the space they have available. Hayyiz brings those two paths into one marketplace.

What I built

  • Developed web and mobile features for renters and owners.
  • Integrated authentication and application data so users could access their accounts and complete their respective workflows.
  • Translated business requirements into features for listing and finding spaces, and tested the resulting user journeys with the team.

Testing and release

I helped debug the web and mobile apps and prepare the Android and iOS betas. I deployed the website to Saudi-hosted servers and configured the domain and DNS.

Working with the team

I worked in a four-person founding team that covered development, design, business planning, and relationships with property owners.
