Wasel

A safety prototype that connects a GPS wearable to a mobile app so caregivers and group leaders can monitor location and receive alerts.

Project on GitHub ↗ Download slides (PPTX) ↓
Project
Team hackathon prototype
Award
2nd place, Asaqefah Sandbox Hackathon
Stack
React Native, Expo, ESP32, ArcGIS

The problem

Caregivers and group leaders can lose track of a child, an older adult, or a visitor in crowded environments. Wasel explores how a wearable and a phone can make location and alerts easier to access.

What I built

  • Integrated an Expo mobile app with an ESP32-based GPS device to show live location.
  • Implemented geofencing so the app could flag when the wearer left a designated area.
  • Added an SOS flow and integrated Esri and ArcGIS for mapping and geographic visualization.

How the prototype works

A caregiver pairs the wearable with the app, views the latest location on a map, and sets a safe area. The prototype surfaces an alert when the wearable leaves that area or the wearer triggers SOS.

Hackathon result

Our team placed second at the Asaqefah Sandbox Hackathon. The slides describe the prototype and concepts the team explored for future versions.
